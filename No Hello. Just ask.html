Please don't just say hello.

Say hi, then ask your question in the same message. It saves everyone time and a lot of waiting around.

Created by @loukizo

Why a lone "Hello" is a problem

When someone sends only a greeting, the person on the other end has no idea what comes next. Is it a five-second question or a long problem? Is it urgent? They can't start thinking about an answer until you reply, and you might not reply for a while.

Both people end up stuck waiting on each other. The conversation that could have been finished in one exchange now takes several, spread across hours.
